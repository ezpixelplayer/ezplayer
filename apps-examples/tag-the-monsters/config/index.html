Beam coverage

The whole group is one pattern lease; the player evaluates the beam per node, on the GPU when it has one. A group of "everything but the matrices" works well.

Sprite surfaces

Where the monsters and friends run, as RGBA sprites over the show that keeps playing: matrices, and any other model with at least 500 nodes and a 2D render buffer (an HD spinner, a big tree), drawn onto that buffer. Each gets an exclusive lease while a round runs.

Wand calibration

The wand reports position in meters and angles in degrees in its own frame: x to the right, z toward the holder's back, yaw 0 facing the display, pitch up. These map that frame onto the layout.
Leave the origin blank to stand in front of the layout's middle.
