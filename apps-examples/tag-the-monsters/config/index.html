Beam coverage

The whole group is one pattern lease; the player evaluates the beam per node, on the GPU when it has one. A group of "everything but the matrices" works well.

Sprite matrices

Grid models the monsters and friends run across, as RGBA sprites over the show that keeps playing. Each gets an exclusive lease while a round runs.

Wand calibration

The wand reports position in meters and angles in degrees in its own frame: x to the right, z toward the holder's back, yaw 0 facing the display, pitch up. These map that frame onto the layout.
Leave the origin blank to stand in front of the layout's middle. The player's layout is in xLights world units; 100 per meter is typical when the layout was drawn in centimeters.
