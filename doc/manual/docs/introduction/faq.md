# Frequently Asked Questions

## What is EZPlayer?

EZPlayer runs pixel light shows. It schedules and plays sequences created with
[xLights](https://xlights.org/) or purchased from [EZRGB](https://ezrgb.com/),
and sends the pixel data to your controllers. See
[What is EZPlayer](/) for the longer version.

## How do I get EZPlayer?

Download it from [EZRGB](https://ezrgb.com/ezplayer), or get source and binary
releases from [GitHub](https://github.com/ezpixelplayer/ezplayer/releases).
EZPlayer runs on Windows, macOS, and Linux (x64 and Raspberry Pi).

## How do I learn more about the features?

It's free, so the fastest way is to download it and try it. This manual covers
everything from [first run](/basics/getting-started-local) to
[advanced scheduling](/advanced/complex-schedules/overview), and the
[README](https://github.com/ezpixelplayer/ezplayer#readme) has a short overview.

## How do I control my show remotely?

Sign up for a free account at [EZRGB.com](https://ezrgb.com/login-register) and
[register your player](/cloud/registering). A URL for your player then appears
under the [EZPlayer section of your account](https://ezrgb.com/my-account/ezplayer).

You can also control EZPlayer over your home network without a cloud account
using the [local web interface](/basics/local-web-interface).

## Where do I get sequences?

[EZRGB](https://ezrgb.com/shop/sequences), [xlights.info](https://xlights.info/),
[Sequence Scout](https://sequencescout.com/browse), and anywhere else fine
sequences are sold. See [Getting sequences](/cloud/getting-sequences) for
loading purchased sequences through the cloud.

## Do I need to use EZRGB?

No. EZPlayer runs completely stand-alone, similar to
[xSchedule](https://github.com/xLightsSequencer/xSchedule) or
[FPP](https://github.com/FalconChristmas/fpp). The cloud features are optional;
see [Privacy](/introduction/privacy) for what is and isn't shared.
